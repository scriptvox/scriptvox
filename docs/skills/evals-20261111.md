## How to read this

- "Fault" is a fault of the skill text: a wrong line, a step that fails, or a thing a user needs that the skill does
  not say. Each fault gives the line, what happened, and a corrected line.
- "Product fault" is a fault of the tool that the eval found. It is listed apart, because the skill text is not wrong.
- The word `rawivox` or `RAWIVOX_` was searched in all 12 skills: 0 hits.
- The installed binary has every flag of 10 and 11 Nov (`--storyboard`, `--until`, `--cover-prompt`, `--background`,
  `--alpha`, `--enhance`, `--bleep`, `--beats`, `--preview`). So a skill that does not name one is a skill fault.

Written 7 Nov 2026 before the skill. Read in full: BaoCut `translate-subtitles` and `narration` (Community License, source-available). `subtitle-workflow` and OpenChatCut `voice` were fetched, not read. Ideas only. No text kept open while the skill was written.

## What the sources teach (our words)

- Text for a screen or a voice is not prose. A good essay in the new language runs long, moves clauses, and falls off the speaker's time.
- One source sentence gives one new sentence. Never join, split, drop or reorder.
- Among equally natural wordings, take the one whose clauses come in the source's order, as an interpreter does.
- Length is part of being right. Fewer words first: drop fillers, spare links and repeated subjects. But never drop a "not", a number, a limit, a name, or a cause and effect link.
- Read the whole text before the first line. Fix the terms and the names and use one form throughout. A user's named term goes in exactly.
- After a small change, redo only what changed, so words the user has read stay the same.
- Measure the voice on a real sample before you count words. A rate table is only a first guess, and a new voice or language needs a new measure.
- One voice for the whole video. Do not fix a long script by speed.
- Report what was measured apart from what a person must hear.

- It states what no check here can prove: mouth match and how the voice sounds.

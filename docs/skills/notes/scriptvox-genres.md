## Where each rule comes from

1. The shape of each kind (what the first scene must do, how it ends) is common knowledge of the craft, written from memory in our words: a news report leads with the event and names who says so; an ad has one promise and one action; a tutorial shows the plan before the first step.
2. The card for each kind comes from what the cards can do today: `steps` lights the spoken step, a table `mark` moves a light through rows, a `number_line` marker travels, `sources_card` names the publishers.
3. The numbers (about 30 words for 15 s, 12 lines of 44 characters, 2.5 s of outro) are from our specs and the real runs.
4. What a recipe cannot do is said in the skill: no picture, no free Arabic voice, no reading of a product page.

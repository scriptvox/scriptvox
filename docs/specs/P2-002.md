# P2-002: generative job runner and `scriptvox generate`

> 5 Nov 2026: `--commercial` is deleted (`docs/specs/commercial-note.md`). Text below about it is history.

- `src/genjob.rs` runs one clip through the fal queue. Money rules: reserve the estimate in the ledger first; write a
  `.submitting` marker, submit, write the job record (request id and the returned status and result URLs), remove
  the marker. A rerun with a job record polls that job again and never submits twice. A marker without a record is
  an unconfirmed charge (exit 6): the fix line says to check the dashboard and how to submit again on purpose. A
  4xx refusal before the job exists releases the reservation (401 or 403 is exit 10, a key problem). The cost is
  committed when the clip is saved.
- Polling uses the returned `status_url` (fal answers 405 to a status URL built from the full model path), slower
  over time (2 s up to 10 s). Past `--wait-s` the job stays on record and the exit is 9.
- The video URL must be on one of the row's `hosts`; the download is the resumable `.part` download of the stock rows.
- `scriptvox generate --model <row> --prompt "..." [--image f] [--seconds N] [--aspect 9:16]`: plan with `plan_id`
  (the image counted by its hash), exit 4 without `--approve`, `--plan` to price only, `--max-spend` as the cap,
  `--commercial` refuses rows whose terms do not allow it. The run id comes from the plan id (and
  `--idempotency-key`), so the same command resumes the same job.
- Rows gain `duration_string` (Kling takes "5") and `extra` (fixed inputs; Kling gets `generate_audio = false`,
  because scriptvox adds its own voice).
- Tests (`tests/generate.rs`, a local fake fal queue): no submit before approval; one submit, clip saved, cost
  committed, a rerun sends nothing; a busy job exits 9 and the rerun finishes it with no second submit; a submit with
  no answer is exit 6 twice with one submit; a refused key is exit 10 and the next run may submit.
- First real call, 2 Nov 2026, with the owner's approval: Kling 2.6 Pro, 5 s, 9:16, $0.35. fal finished in about
  5 minutes; the first download failed on a local DNS error, the rerun resumed the same job and saved the clip. The
  ledger shows one commit of $0.35. The clip: 1080x1920, 24 fps, 5.04 s, no audio track. Verified by this call: the
  download host `fal.media`, `duration` as a string, `generate_audio = false`.
- Free self-hosted rows (gateway `selfhost`, $0): a job that finishes with an error removes its record and ends with
  exit 5 (retryable), so the same command submits again. Paid rows keep exit 6 and the record (3 Nov 2026: a failed
  BiRefNet load blocked the rerun until the run folder was deleted).

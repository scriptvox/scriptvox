# P2-006: clip checks for generated clips

`gates::clip_check` runs on every generated clip before it is used (`generate`, and generated scenes in `make`):
it decodes; it is at least 80% of the asked length; the short side is at least 320 px (a frame that is not the
asked aspect is a warning: scriptvox crops it); at most 30% black (blackdetect); at most 50% frozen (freezedetect).
A fail is exit 3 with the findings. The clip was paid for, so it stays in the run folder, and the fix line says to
change the prompt or the model (a new plan). `generate` without FFmpeg reports "not checked" as a warning, never a
silent pass. Test: a 2 s still clip fails `clip_length` and `clip_motion` and is kept.

## A clip from a picture (G155)

A failed `clip_motion` on a clip from a picture says that a flat picture may not move on this row, and points to `media` with `camera`. No fix line says "paid": the row can be free.

`plan` and `make` warn with `picture_shape` when a `generate.image` is of another shape than the clip, so less than 90 percent of it stays.

## Findings that give a reason (G158)

A `words` line with 5 or more heard words that are not in the script says how many are not in the script. The usual cause is a false repeat from speech to text. A row named in a brief that does not fit says which reason: wrong kind, shape or no image to video. A card kind `pie` offers `donut`.

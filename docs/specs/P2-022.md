# P2-022: `scriptvox edit` (cut silence, reframe)

- `scriptvox edit <video> --cut-silence [--min-silence 0.6] [--pad 0.15] [--noise-db -35]`: FFmpeg silencedetect,
  keep the parts between pauses, each grown by `pad` into the pause, touching parts merged, parts under 0.1 s dropped;
  one select/aselect pass. A video with no audio, or silent at that level, is refused with a fix line.
- `--reframe 9:16|16:9|1:1`: scale to cover and centre crop to the scriptvox frame (1080x1920 for 9:16). Subject
  tracking waits for a detector from the open-model lab.
- Local and free: no plan, no approval. Output `scriptvox-out/<name>-edit.mp4`; metrics `seconds_before`,
  `seconds_after`, `cuts`, `size`.
- Tests: unit tests for the keep list and the log parser; a 6 s test video with 2 s of silence in the middle comes
  out about 4.3 s, one cut, 1080x1920, and the file length matches the reported length.
- Idea from OpenMontage (silence cutter, auto reframe), our own code.

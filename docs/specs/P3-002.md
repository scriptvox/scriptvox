# P3-002: open voices in `make` (Chatterbox + Whisper on self-hosted GPUs)

> 5 Nov 2026: `--commercial` is deleted (`docs/specs/commercial-note.md`). Text below about it is history.

- Voice rows `selfhost/chatterbox-ar` and `selfhost/chatterbox-en` (registry/voices.toml): free, MIT, network, key `selfhost`.
  Picked with `--tts selfhost` (or `tts` in the brief), and automatically under `--commercial` when no other free voice
  allows commercial use. One built-in voice; a consented sample can be cloned later.
- `src/opentts.rs`: the text is split into whole-sentence chunks (at most 220 characters); each chunk is spoken by the
  `tts` row and transcribed by the `stt` row; the audio is cut 0.15 s after the word where the transcript reaches
  the script's letter count (models sometimes invent words after the text: seen on 2 Nov, 2.7 s of made-up speech);
  the script's own words are laid on the transcript times by letter position. Captions always show the script.
- All calls go through the job runner (job files in the segment folder, so a rerun reuses finished chunks). A free
  row may resubmit after a lost answer: nothing can be charged twice.
- The voice cache key carries `opentts::VERSION`, so a pipeline change never brings back an old voice.
- Not done: the fitter's rate change has no effect on this voice (atempo still fits small gaps); a DTW alignment
  for runs of misheard words.
- Measured 2 Nov 2026: an 18-word Arabic script with a pause, 8.6 s video, voice in about 19 s, $0; captions right
  to left, word highlight in time (contact sheet checked by eye).

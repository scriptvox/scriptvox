# P3-003: `scriptvox analyze`

`scriptvox analyze <video> [--threshold 0.25] [--transcript] [--lang ar]` reads a video before you copy its style:
duration, size, fps; shots from FFmpeg scene detection on frames sampled at 4 fps (a scriptvox crossfade scores about
0.09 per frame but 0.4 between samples 0.25 s apart; a moving shot with no cut stays near 0.08); shot count, mean,
shortest, longest, cuts per minute; loudness (LUFS) and silence share; `shot_list`; a strip of one frame per shot (up to 4 in a row, more in a grid)
(at most 12) as `shots_image`. `--transcript` sends 16 kHz mono audio to a speech to text row (the free selfhost Whisper
row) and adds the text and words per minute. Idea from OpenMontage's video analyzer; our own code.
Tests: unit tests for cuts and stats; a 6 s video with a hard cut at 3 s gives 2 shots, cut within 0.3 s.
Measured on the two-scene open video: 2 shots (cut at 3.25 s), -14.0 LUFS, 169 words a minute, transcript 17 of 17
words exact.

## Sentences (3 Nov)

`transcript.sentences`: start, end and text per sentence, from the timed words. A sentence ends at a word ending in
. ? ! or the Arabic question mark, or before a pause over 1.2 s. So an agent picks a 20 to 60 s clip without the word
list (55 s video: 15 sentences in 1.5 KB, the word file 12 KB). Measured: English and Arabic talk clips split right
(2 sentences each); clip factory test on a 55 s narrated video: the agent took 21.44 to 45.42 s from the sentences,
`edit --from 21.3 --to 45.6` gave a 24.3 s clip whose transcript starts 0.14 s before "Today" and ends on "later."
(`night-output/clipfactory/`). Test: `sentences_from_words` (punctuation, pause, Arabic, empty).

## Cut count under load (3 Nov)

A test once found 1 shot instead of 2 under load. Likely cause, not reproduced in 40 parallel runs: the audio and
video filters log from two threads, and `ebur128` writes its line per 100 ms in pieces; a `showinfo` line that lands
inside one loses its `[Parsed_showinfo` prefix, which `parse_cuts` required. Fix: `ebur128=framelog=quiet` (only the
summary), and `parse_cuts` takes any line with `pts_time:` (only showinfo prints it). Unit test with a mixed line.

# P3-005: first frames in briefs

> 5 Nov 2026: `--commercial` is deleted (`docs/specs/commercial-note.md`). Text below about it is history.

A `generate` scene may carry `first_frame: "<picture prompt>"` (or `image`, a file; not both). The planner picks an
image row with a key that fits the aspect and `--commercial`, and a video row with `i2v`. At render time the image row
makes `scene<N>_frame.png` in the run folder, then the video row animates it. Both are job-runner calls, so a rerun
reuses both. The plan id covers the image row and its prompt. No image row with a key: exit 10 with a fix line.
Test: a fake queue sees two submits (image, then image to video) and the video renders.

Measured 2 Nov 2026: a two-scene Arabic brief, both scenes `first_frame` (FLUX.2 klein) then Wan i2v, voice
`--tts selfhost`: 6.8 s video, $0, about 8 minutes of GPU time in all. Frames fit the captions; one fake name painted on
the dhow hull (the known text risk). Found on the way: a first frame of 1.8 MB passed the 2 MB ingress limit once
base64 encoded (HTTP 413); the video ingress now allows 64 MB, like the audio one.

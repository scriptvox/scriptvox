# P3-007: generated music beds (ACE-Step on self-hosted GPUs)

> 5 Nov 2026: `--commercial` is deleted (`docs/specs/commercial-note.md`). Text below about it is history.

- Engine `acestep-xl-turbo/music` in the video image of `services/gen`: ACE-Step v1.5 XL Turbo (MIT, diffusers 0.42.1
  `AceStepPipeline`), 8 steps, stereo 48 kHz, empty lyrics give an instrumental. Row `acestep-xl-turbo-self`
  (`music.gen`, free, durations 15/30/60/120 s).
- Brief field `music_prompt` (not together with `music`). The planner picks a music row with a key that fits
  `--commercial`, adds a `music` stage, and the plan id covers the prompt and the row. After the voice sets the video
  length, the row makes a bed 10 s longer than the video (a piece can fade out early), and the master mixes it under
  the voice and trims it. `--local-only` or no key: exit 10 with the built-in bed as the alternative.
- Measured 2 Nov 2026: 15 s bed in 5.7 s; the spectrum shows instruments and percussion, but the piece faded out
  after about 9.5 s of 15 (hence the margin). An 8.6 s Arabic video with the open voice and a generated bed: master
  at -14.0 LUFS.
- Tests: plan shows the music stage; `music` plus `music_prompt` is exit 2; no key is exit 10.

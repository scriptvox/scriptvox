# P3-008: scriptvox judge (a vision model scores frames against the brief)

Gap: OpenMontage and HyperFrames leave the final look to the agent's own eyes. An agent that cannot see video (or
that misreads a 3x3 sheet) ships a clip whose picture does not match the script. ScriptVox now asks an open vision
model, per frame, and gives back scores and fixes in the envelope. The rubric and the prompt are ScriptVox's own.

- `scriptvox judge <run|file> [--brief f] [--frames 6] [--endpoint URL] [--model M] [--min-score 3]`.
- Frames: `--frames` evenly spaced (mid points, as `snapshot --sheet`), each a JPEG 768 px wide, with its time and the
  caption on screen then (the run's SRT).
- Brief: `--brief` (the file's text), else the run's `request.json` (script, hook, scenes). With neither, the model
  judges the picture only.
- One request with all frames to an OpenAI-compatible `/chat/completions` endpoint, JSON schema in
  `response_format` (vLLM guided decoding), temperature 0, thinking off.
  Default: the free self-hosted row `https://vision.example.invalid/qwen38-27b/v1`, model `Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B` (Apache-2.0, native
  vision), no key. Override with `--endpoint`/`--model`. The stored `vision` key is sent only to the configured URL, over https or on loopback; any other `--endpoint` gets no key (audit security-2).
- Per frame: `score` 1 to 5 (5: matches the brief and looks finished), `matches_brief`, `text_ok` (on-screen text
  readable, not cut off, same words as the caption), and `issues`. Overall: `score` and up to 5 `fixes`.
- Envelope: `metrics.judge_score`, `metrics.judge_min`, `frames` (t, caption, score, issues), `findings` (one per frame
  under `--min-score`, with the frame's fix), `next` (the fixes), `artifacts.frames` (the JPEG folder).
  Exit 3 (gate failed) when any frame scores under `--min-score`; exit 5 when the endpoint fails or times out
  (retry is safe: nothing is charged).
- Advisory, not part of `make`: it needs the network and a model, and `make` gates stay local and free.
- Tests: the reply parser (good JSON, fenced JSON, frame count mismatch, out of range scores) and an end to end run
  against a fake local endpoint (`tests/judge.rs`) that returns one weak frame and gives exit 3 with a finding.
- Behind titles (3 Nov): the rubric says a `"behind": true` title is meant to sit behind the subject and asks only
  whether its words read. Before: every behind title scored 3 ("overlaps the subject") and an unreadable one passed
  with `text_ok` true. After: the unreadable "ABU DHABI" video fails (2, `text_ok` false), the readable "CITY" video
  passes, the same in two runs each.

# RVX-005: encoder probe, aspects and templates

- Encoder probe (`engine_ffmpeg::pick_encoder`): a 1-frame test encode per candidate before the real render. Default candidates are CPU only: libx264, then libopenh264 (for LGPL FFmpeg builds). `SCRIPTVOX_ENCODER=auto` tries h264_nvenc, h264_qsv, h264_amf and h264_videotoolbox first, and any other value is an explicit list. Every skipped encoder and the chosen one appear in `explain`. Idea from MoneyPrinterTurbo `app/services/video.py` (MIT: try, then fall back to libx264). Ours probes first instead of failing a render, and it is CPU-first by default (owner rule: no local GPU work).
- BT.709 colour tags on every encode. Atomic output: render to `<out>.part.mp4`, then rename.
- `make --aspect 9:16|16:9|1:1`. Caption size scales from the short side (84 px at 1080). Lines are capped at about 42 characters on wide frames. The bottom margin is 19% of height on tall frames and 9% on others.
- Captions: the no-orphan rule now runs at every page end (sentence end, time limit, gap or full page).
- `scriptvox templates`: narrated-gradient and kinetic-text are available. own-media-reel (RVX-013) and stock-short (RVX-021) are listed as planned.
- Test: `SCRIPTVOX_ENCODER=h264_fake_hw,libx264` falls back, `explain` says why, and no `.part.mp4` is left.

Deferred: the `title` and `image-pan` goldens move to RVX-013, where stills and Ken Burns arrive with own media.

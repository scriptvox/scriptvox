# RVX-007: captions

- `align(script, events)`: script words (with punctuation) get TTS word times.
- `paginate`: greedy lines by measured width, at most `max_lines` per page, new page at a sentence end, a silence over `max_gap`, or a page longer than `max_page` (4 s for shorts). Orphan rules: the last word of a sentence never sits alone on a page or on a line.
- `Measurer`: harfrust shaping, scaled like libass (`Fontsize` = OS/2 win ascent plus win descent). `tests/captions_render.rs` compares it with a real libass render: 938/934 px (Latin), 604/602 px (Arabic).
- Output: ASS (`WrapStyle: 2`, explicit `\N`, `\k` karaoke, script text cannot inject override tags), SRT and VTT. Snapshots for EN and AR. A proptest checks no word is lost, pages stay in order, and the line limit holds.
- Goldens: real edge-tts 7.3.1 word timings in `fixtures/golden/captions-{en,ar}/words.json`; `captions-ar/frame_8s.png` is a rendered check.

Deferred: `schemas/caption.json` comes with the `schema` command (RVX-025). Mixed-script lines use one font per style; libass falls back inside `fontsdir` for the other script (spike row 1).

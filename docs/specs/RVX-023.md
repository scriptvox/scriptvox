# RVX-023: music beds, ducking and loudness

Open question Q10 (which CC0 music beds) is resolved this way: ScriptVox synthesises its beds with FFmpeg `aevalsrc` recipes at render time. There is no third-party audio, no asset file and no licence question. Users can still pass their own music file.

- `make --music calm|pulse|<file in the project>|none` (default none). `calm` is an A minor pad that breathes every 4 s. `pulse` is a low 100 BPM pulse under a fifth. A file outside the project gives `SVX_PATH_OUTSIDE_ROOT`. A file loops to the video length.
- Ducking (idea: RM #3102, declined there): `sidechaincompress` with the narration as the sidechain (threshold 0.04, ratio 3, attack 15 ms, release 450 ms; changed 5 Nov 2026, see `music-level.md`). The bed fades in and out over 1 s.
- Master (`src/audio.rs`): the mix goes to `mix.wav`. Pass 1 measures with `loudnorm print_format=json`. Pass 2 normalises linearly to -14 LUFS integrated, true peak -1.5 dBTP, giving `master.wav`. It applies to every video, with or without music. Edge voices arrive near -22 LUFS.
- The video's audio track is the master (encoded once, at the final mux). `artifacts.master` points to it.
- Live (own media, calm bed): -14.0 LUFS, true peak -1.4 dBFS after AAC.
- Tests (`tests/audio.rs`): none, calm and pulse each land within 1 LU of -14 LUFS. The duck chain lowers the bed by 6 dB or more while the voice speaks (measured with `astats` in two windows).

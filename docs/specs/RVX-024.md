# RVX-024: quality gates, check and snapshot

Ideas: OM #358 (only 4 frames were sampled), HF #5479, RM #7099, RM #5913. The rubric is ScriptVox's own.

- `src/gates.rs`: one FFmpeg pass over every frame and sample (`blackdetect`, `freezedetect`, `silencedetect`, `ebur128`), plus ffprobe stream lengths. Gates:
  - `black`: a black stretch of 2 s or more fails;
  - `slideshow`: the frozen share of the video, over 50% fails and over 30% warns. A freeze that lasts to the end of the file (start, no duration) counts to the end. A test found this case;
  - `silence`: 4 s or more warns;
  - `loudness`: more than 2 LU from -14 LUFS warns, and no audio warns;
  - `av_drift`: stream lengths that differ by more than one frame fail.
  Each finding has a status, a detail and a fix.
- `scriptvox check <run|file>`: exit 3 when a gate fails, with the first failure's fix as the envelope fix.
- `scriptvox snapshot <run|file> [--at 1,5] [--sheet N] [--out f]`: one PNG (up to 4 frames side by side, more in a grid, or an N-frame grid; 9 by default), so an agent can Read it.
- `make` runs the gates at the end. A failure gives exit 3, the video is kept, and the findings are in the envelope.
- Tests (`tests/gates.rs`): 30 s of black gives exit 3 with a fix; 5 s of video with 3 s of audio fails `av_drift`; a frozen colour fails `slideshow`; the gradient template and own media (with Ken Burns stills) pass; `--sheet 9` writes a PNG.

Audit fix, 9 Nov 2026: `check` exits 2 on a file with no video stream, a failed FFmpeg read, a decode error in the log, or a decode that stops more than 0.5 s before the shortest stream's stated end. A file with no sound has no `silence` finding, since nothing was examined.

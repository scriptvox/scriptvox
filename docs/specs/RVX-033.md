# RVX-033: bench, recipes and launch assets

- `scriptvox bench`: the hardware (OS, arch, CPU model, cores, RAM), the caption engine (2,000 Arabic words shaped and paged) and the full demo render (8 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps, with the realtime factor). It writes `scriptvox-out/bench-<date>.json`. On this machine (i9-12900, debug build): demo at 5.4x real time, captions in 195 ms.
- 6 recipes in `recipes/` (from RVX-025): Arabic explainer, own-media reel, stock short, timed 30 s, wide explainer, square quiet. Each is a valid brief (tested).
- `docs/launch/`: hero scripts in English and Arabic (`hero-en.json`, `hero-ar.json`) and a status table.
- `just release-check [version]`: runs CI, then fails while a launch asset, a release checklist or `CHANGELOG.md` is missing, or `Cargo.toml` is not at the version.

Not done, and why:
- The hero videos need a voice that allows commercial use. The plan says no launch asset may use the Edge voice, and Q7 (hero voice) and Q8 (demo narration) are open. ElevenLabs needs a key, and the owner's own recording needs the owner.
- The GIFs need a terminal recorder (VHS) on the build machine.
- Recipes as low-resolution goldens need a deterministic voice. The stub voice tests cover the pipeline.

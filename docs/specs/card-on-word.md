# A card lands on a spoken word: `on` and `hold` (10 Nov 2026)

Before: a scene card showed from the first frame of its scene to the last. A number often belongs to one word ("revenue grew by forty two percent"): the stat was on screen two seconds before the voice reached it. A slice of GAPS row G058 (card timing); the idea is ours from a one-line note that an overlay can start at a chosen moment, no other tool's code or docs were read.

- **Rule.** Card field `on`, a word of the scene's narration, now works on every card kind: the card comes in when the voice says that word. Before it the scene shows its picture or background with the captions only.
- **Matching.** The same test the formula card uses: the first spoken word of the scene that starts with `on`, both folded by `gates::norm_word` (case, punctuation, Arabic letter forms and diacritics, "four" equals "4"). Write the word as the script writes it: `forty` does not find "42", and a bare Arabic word does not find the same word with a joined و.
- **Refusal.** `plan` and `make` refuse an `on` word the scene's script does not have, with the word and the scene number (exit 2), and an `on` with no letter or digit. If the voice's word list still lacks it at render, `make` stops with the same kind of message. No silent fallback.
- **Limits.** The card never starts before its scene's own start (the wait for the hook stays), and a word in the last 0.9 s of the scene starts the card 0.9 s before the cut so it is seen.
- **`hold`**, seconds, a number above 0: how long the card stays after it comes in, with or without `on`. Then it leaves with its usual fade and the scene goes on. Default: to the scene's end. `plan` refuses 0, a negative, a value that is not a number, and a hold no voice could reach (over 4 s a word plus 10 s plus the script's pauses; a `duration` that stretches a very short scene past that is refused too, a known limit). The real scene length is known only after the voice is made, so a `hold` past the scene's end stops at the end. A `hold` under 0.5 s shows for 0.5 s. A card's own limit still wins: a title goes after 2.5 s, a lower third after 4 s.
- **What each kind does.** `cards::window` gives the card's start and end; `events`, `landed_in`, `cards.json` (`start`, `end`, `landed`) and the matte window of a `behind` title all take those. Every kind's motion counts from its start, so it moves with it: the stat count, bar and timeline stagger, the line and plot draw, code typing, the donut, the quote and compare fades. `snapshot --cards` reads `landed`, so it shows the card after it came in. `text_bounds` and `captions` read the burned events, which carry the new times.
- **List.** An item spoken before the `on` word appears at once when the card lands; later items still wait for their words. An item spoken after a `hold` has ended is not waited for: it comes in the even spread inside the card's time.
- **Formula exception.** For a formula with `then`, `on` keeps its meaning: the moment the first formula turns into the second, and a word that is not found still means halfway (see `formula-morph.md`, unchanged). Such a card takes no `hold`. A formula without `then` still refuses `on`. A formula that arrives late needs another field: not done.
- **Old briefs.** With neither field `window` returns the scene's times untouched, so the events are byte for byte the same; the fields are not written when absent, so plan ids do not change.

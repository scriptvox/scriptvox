# Brief `emphasis`: key words in the captions (5 Nov 2026)

Gap: the caption rules in HyperFrames' skills ask for emphasis words, with code per video. ScriptVox lit every word the same.

- Brief `emphasis`: words or phrases of the script. Case and punctuation do not count, and Arabic letter forms compare as in the words gate. Every place the script says one is marked.
- `karaoke`, `pop`: accent colour before the word is said, and 112 percent size. Colour alone was lost, because karaoke paints every said word in the accent by the end of the page (seen by eye).
- `reveal`: the word stays in the accent and does not turn white.
- An entry the script does not say gives the `emphasis` warning at `plan` and in the result.
- `resume --brief` takes it from the new brief, like the other look fields. `edit --captions --emphasis` takes a comma list; words Whisper did not hear come back in `captions.emphasis_not_heard`.

Checked by eye (drafts): English karaoke, pop, reveal; Arabic pop; a 16:9 line with every word marked stays inside the frame and its box.

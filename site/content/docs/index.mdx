## Install

```sh
curl -fsSL https://github.com/scriptvox/scriptvox/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
```

Other routes (Homebrew, Scoop, `.deb`, Windows) are in the [README](https://github.com/scriptvox/scriptvox#installation).

## Your first video

```sh
scriptvox demo --out demo.mp4
```

This needs no key and no account. It makes a short narrated, captioned video on your machine.

## Check your machine

```sh
scriptvox doctor
```
